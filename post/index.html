No Percolation at Criticality: the θ(p_c) = 0 Proof, Visualized

Open each edge of a lattice independently with probability p. Above a critical value pc an infinite open cluster appears. But is there one exactly at pc? This is the θ(pc) = 0 problem of percolation theory. It was settled in two dimensions by Harris and Kesten, and in high dimensions by the lace expansion, but stayed open for dimensions 3 to 10 for decades.
